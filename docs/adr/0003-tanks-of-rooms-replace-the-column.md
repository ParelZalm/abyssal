# Tanks of rooms replace the open water column

The run was one open column, 9000 units deep, descended by growing: body size opened each
thermocline, set the zoom, and decided who swallowed whom. Its pacing problem was
structural — with size the only gate, the best play was to grind the easy water — and a
string of systems existed only to patch it (the shallows clock, the pocket under a seal,
forcing a seal, hunger). The decision layer stayed thin however many were added.

So the run is now a chain of **tanks** inside an aquarium, each a grid of one-screen
**rooms** in the manner of *The Binding of Isaac*: fights that lock their exits, a
treasure room, a shop, a deal room, a boss who is the only way on. Power comes from
mutations on pedestals, not from a level-up draft, and growth happens at the **descent**
between tanks — each tank is authored at its animal's scale, so the body looks the same
size on screen and the fourfold zoom change that killed two background attempts is gone.
The view stays side-on: an aquarium is looked into through the glass, and the corals,
algae and wrecks the rooms are built from read in profile and not from above.

What survives is what made the game itself: the genome split, organs, synergies and
transformations, menace, blood, and swallowing — which no longer turns on size alone but
on a bite that would have killed.

## Considered and rejected

- **Keeping growth as the spine**, with rooms cutting up the column. It keeps the grind,
  and a room built for a hatchling cannot hold the same body at 200 cm.
- **A top-down camera**, as Isaac has. It would undo the side-on pixel pass for a gain
  water does not need: with no gravity, a side-on plane plays like Isaac's floor.
- **Mouse aiming.** Isaac's split — move on WASD, attack on the arrows — is what lets you
  kite; the old cursor steering was a chase control.
